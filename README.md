# suguru-artifacts
# SHIBUYA // RECOVERY ARCHIVE

**October 31, 2018**

I don't know who will find this.

I don't know if anyone is supposed to.

The files were recovered separately. Some were damaged. Some had already been deleted. Others were left exactly where they were, as though whoever created them expected someone to come looking.

At first, I thought they were simply records of the incident.

I was wrong.

There are too many references to the same person.

**Suguru Geto.**

That name appears in photographs, reports, timestamps, handwritten notes, and correspondence recovered from the area.

That shouldn't be possible.

Geto died years ago.

Everyone knows that.

And yet, on the night of October 31st, 2018, people saw him.

Not a photograph.

Not a recording.

Him.

The same face.

The same scars.

The same voice.

The same body.

That is what bothered me most.

The body.

I kept coming back to that word while sorting through the files.

A body can be identified.

A face can be identified.

A name can be identified.

But those three things don't necessarily belong to the same person.

---

## 23:17

The first photograph was taken in Shibuya.

I don't know who took it.

The metadata says otherwise.

It lists the artist as **Suguru Geto**.

That would be meaningless on its own. Metadata can be changed. Names can be forged. Files can be copied.

But the coordinates are real.

The photograph was taken in Shibuya.

The date is real too.

**October 31, 2018.**

I checked it several times.

There are no obvious anomalies in the photograph itself. Nothing that immediately explains why someone would preserve it.

Then I found the comment.

> *This is my body, but it isn't. The face is familiar. The name is not.*

I read it more times than I care to admit.

At first I thought it was some kind of code.

Then I thought it was a message from Geto.

Now I'm not sure what would be worse.

---

## 23:42

I started looking through the records from Shibuya.

The more I found, the less sense they made.

Gojo was there.

The Prison Realm was there.

The barriers were there.

And Geto was there.

That last part should have been impossible.

There are records of people reacting to him. There are accounts describing his appearance. There are references to the scars across his forehead.

Nobody seems to question whether it is actually him.

They recognize the face.

That is the problem.

Everyone recognizes the face.

Nobody recognizes the person.

---

## 00:08

I found an older report.

It describes a conversation involving Geto.

Or someone who looked like Geto.

The report repeatedly refers to him by name, but there is something strange about the wording.

It never says:

**"Suguru Geto said..."**

It says:

**"The individual identifying himself as Suguru Geto..."**

I thought that distinction was insignificant.

It isn't.

---

## 00:31

There is another photograph.

Same face.

Different context.

Same body.

Different name.

I don't understand how both records can be genuine.

Unless one of them isn't.

Unless neither of them is.

---

## 01:14

I searched the name.

Suguru Geto.

Everything leads back to the same conclusion.

Dead.

The records agree.

The witnesses agree.

The history agrees.

So why was someone wearing his face?

I kept thinking about the message from the first photograph.

*The face is familiar.*

That part makes sense.

*The name is not.*

That makes sense too.

But the first sentence is the one I can't explain.

*This is my body, but it isn't.*

Whose body?

Who is speaking?

And why would they describe Geto's body as their own?

---

## 02:03

I found the word **Kenjaku**.

I don't know if that is a name.

An alias.

A title.

Or something else entirely.

It appears in older records alongside descriptions of bodies that should have belonged to other people.

Different eras.

Different faces.

The same implication.

A person does not always remain attached to the body they were born with.

I don't know how much of it is true.

At this point, I'm not sure truth is particularly useful.

---

## 02:47

There is one thing that keeps appearing throughout the archive.

A distinction between **body** and **self**.

Who you are.

What you look like.

What your body remembers.

What other people remember you as.

I think that's why Geto was useful.

Everyone knew his face.

Everyone trusted what they recognized.

They saw Suguru Geto.

They didn't see the person standing behind his eyes.

---

## 03:26

I finally understand the photograph.

Or at least I think I do.

The photograph isn't really about Shibuya.

The coordinates aren't the answer.

The date isn't the answer.

Even the name in the metadata isn't the answer.

They're all pointing toward the same question.

**Who was actually there?**

The person in the photograph has Geto's face.

The records call him Geto.

But the message says otherwise.

And if the records are correct...

then the person who appeared in Shibuya was not Suguru Geto.

---

## 04:11

I've started moving the files into this archive.

I don't know how long I'll have access to them.

Somebody has already begun removing things.

I found evidence of files being deleted before I ever downloaded them.

There are gaps in the timeline.

Missing photographs.

Missing reports.

Missing names.

Someone doesn't want this documented.

So I'm documenting it anyway.

If you're reading this, then you probably found the photograph first.

If you did, you already have the date.

You already have the location.

You already have the name.

You just need to understand what they mean together.

Don't trust the face.

Don't trust the name.

Look at the body.

---

## 04:39

There is one final thing I should write down.

If you're going to continue through the archive, don't assume that everything here was meant to survive.

Some of these files were deliberately removed.

Some records were rewritten.

Some things only exist because someone forgot that deleting a file is not the same as destroying its history.

The archive remembers.

Git remembers.

People don't always.

---

## 05:02

I think I know who the photograph was referencing now.

I wish I didn't.

The person in the photograph was wearing Suguru Geto's body.

But the person inside it had another name.

**Kenjaku.**

If that answer is correct, then everything else in this archive makes sense.

If it isn't...

then I have spent the entire night following the wrong person.

Either way, there is only one place left to look.

The files that were deleted.
